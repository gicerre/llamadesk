# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in LlamaDesk a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps this project:

- being kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Rust, to Tauri, or to the domain;
- giving feedback on the code, not on the person;
- accepting that the maintainers say no to things that conflict with the project's constraints
  (no network, no telemetry, no hardcoded data), and hearing the reason without taking it
  personally;
- writing in the language you are comfortable with — Italian and English are both fine here.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private;
- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances of any kind;
- publishing other people's private information without permission;
- deliberately derailing discussions or issues.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and
whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer ([@gicerre](https://github.com/gicerre))
through a GitHub private message or a [security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) if you
prefer a confidential channel. Reports are handled discreetly and the reporter's identity is not
shared.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block accounts, in proportion
to the behaviour. Serious or repeated violations lead to a permanent ban from the project.
